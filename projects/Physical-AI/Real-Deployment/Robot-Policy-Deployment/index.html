Real Deployment

Robot Policy Deployment

Collect demonstrations on a real SO-101 arm and turn them into deployable ACT and SmolVLA policies.

Goals

  • Teleoperate a leader-follower SO-101 setup
  • Record a personal LeRobot dataset with two cameras
  • Train and evaluate ACT from scratch
  • Fine-tune and deploy SmolVLA on the same demonstrations

SO-101 Policy Pipeline

What this section covers

Each lab builds on the data and calibration produced by the previous one.

  • RPD01 — Teleoperation

    Connect, calibrate, and teleoperate the leader and follower arms.

  • RPD02 — Data Collection

    Record synchronized robot and camera demonstrations.

  • RPD03 — ACT

    Train ACT from scratch and deploy it on the follower arm.

  • RPD04 — SmolVLA

    Fine-tune SmolVLA and compare it with ACT.
